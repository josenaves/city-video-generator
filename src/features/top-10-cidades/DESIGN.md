# Design System: Piores Enchentes Brasil (Documentary Elite)
**Project ID:** 1484295400628051334

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The design follows a "Netflix Documentary" aesthetic: immersive, emotional, and high-contrast. It utilizes "Obsidian Depth"—a system of dark, layered surfaces with cinematic lighting. The atmosphere is solemn yet premium, emphasizing the scale of the tragedies through depth and professional typography.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Obsidian Deep (#0a0a0b):** Primary background color for immersive depth.
* **Pure Crystal (#ffffff):** Used for primary headings and critical information to ensure maximum readability against dark backgrounds.
* **Muted Silver (#a1a1aa):** Functional role for supporting text and metadata.
* **Emergency Crimson (#dc2626):** (Custom) Used for high-importance metrics like death tolls to convey urgency and impact.
* **Glass Surface (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)):** Secondary role for structural elements, providing a modern glassmorphism feel.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Headings:** 'Outfit' (Extra Bold). Negative tracking (-0.05em) for a punchy, editorial look.
* **Data:** 'Inter' (Bold/Black). High visibility for metrics.
* **Subtitles:** 'Inter' (Medium). 0.2em letter-spacing for an elegant, spaced-out cinematic feel.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Ranking Badge:** Floating silver or gold gradient plates with a 1px rim light (rgba(255,255,255,0.4)). 16px corner roundness.
* **Data Cards:** "Deep Obsidian" glass panels. 32px generously rounded corners. Backdrop-blur of 40px. Heavy, soft diffused shadows for elevation.
* **Metric Items:** Typography-first design. Large, high-contrast numbers with a subtle drop-shadow (0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)).

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Spatial Hierarchy:** Large margins (100px+) to allow the design to "breathe."
* **Depth Layering:** Background Ken Burns effect on images, overlaid with blurred glows, topped by sharp glass cards.
* **Split Layout:** 45/55 distribution between the cinematic visual (portrait/image) and the data panel.
